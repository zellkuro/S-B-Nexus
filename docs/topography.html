Contours every 5 m, bolder every 25 m. Scale: 1 map unit is half a metre, so the coast is about 800 m wide and 500 m deep, and the highest ground, on Hollow Crag and Thornback Ridge, is a little over 80 m. The heights are a starting point: change any of them.
